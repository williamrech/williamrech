Item sprites (gd-*.png): frames of the loot sprites from Flare (https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game),
art by the Flare team, CC-BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
Cropped from the sprite sheets in mods/fantasycore/images/loot, some scaled down.

City strips (gd-city-*.webp): the app owner's own painted Malmouth skyline, cut into rows and compressed.
Raven (gd-raven.webp): drawn for this app (frames of a wingbeat, rendered from code), no third-party art.

Fonts (web/fonts): Cinzel, IM Fell and Risque (risque.woff, a Latin subset renamed "GD Bar"), all under the SIL Open
Font License; see the OFL-*.txt files there.
Target bar and devotion constellations: the bar's gold frame was drawn for this app as vectors (not the game's texture);
the constellations are star coordinates and links from Grim Dawn's game data (no game artwork).
